# Final Report

This folder holds the final deliverables submitted for the CDE3301/IS305 capstone project, along with the working material used to produce them.

## Submission

- **`IS305 - Final Report - Submitted.pdf`**: the final version of the report as submitted for assessment.
- **Demonstration video**: a recorded demonstration of AEGIS in operation, combining the camera's point of view, the operator dashboard, and a third-person view of an operator performing a kitting task. Hosted on YouTube: <https://youtu.be/AxHCu2dXehM>.
- **`media/Workstation Setup.jpg`**: a photograph of the complete physical workstation.

## Supporting Material

- **`sources/`**: photographs, test data, diagrams, and other reference material drawn on while writing the report. See [`sources/README.md`](sources/README.md) for details.
- **`images/`**: diagrams generated for and embedded into the report, such as the electrical architecture and system flowchart figures.
- **Word documents and drafts** (`IS305 - Final Report.docx` and related files): earlier drafts and working versions of the report, retained for reference. The PDF listed above is the authoritative, submitted version.
- **Python scripts** (`embed_diagrams.py`, `generate_diagrams.py`, and others): utility scripts used during report preparation to generate figures and assemble the document. They are not part of the AEGIS system itself.
